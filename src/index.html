Welcome to Chat Archive Viewer

Developed by LynellPhysioDev

Easily browse, search, and view your Facebook and Messenger chat exports through a clean, modern interface.

🔒 100% Local & Private

Your chat logs, photos, and JSON files are processed entirely inside your browser. No personal messages or data are ever uploaded, stored, or sent to external servers.

Step-by-Step Export Instructions

  1. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Select Your Information and Permissions → Export Your Information.
  3. Click Create Export, select your profile, and choose Export to Device.
  4. Set your format to JSON, select your date range, and click Start Export.
  5. Once downloaded, extract the ZIP file and navigate to:
    your_facebook_activity/messages
💡 Quick Link: Direct shortcut to Meta Accounts Centre.

(Messenger's Secure Storage/End-to-End Encryption is required to download conversation)

  1. Open Messenger.com on a computer and click your profile picture.
  2. Go to Privacy & Safety → End-to-End Encrypted Chats.
  3. Select Secure Storage → Download Secure Storage Data.
  4. Click Download File and enter your Facebook password when prompted.
  5. Save and extract the file to view your encrypted message history.
